# 📝 Changelog - Custom Metadata Plugin

Version history, newest first. It replaces the many fix notes written during development (kept outside the repository): each fix appears here in a few lines.

**Numbering:** `0.0.x alpha`, assigned after the fact to match the Git history. The project had no version number: the "1.0.0" to "1.5.0" entries of the old changelogs correspond to 0.0.1 to 0.0.6, and the work done between February and November, never numbered, is grouped as 0.0.7. Git tags are spelled `v0.0.8`. Ongoing changes go under "Unreleased" (rules: [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Unreleased

- **Projection theme: pictures show sooner**: an album's head picture is asked for before the thumbnails and shows in a small size first; the photo viewer shows the thumbnail at once, asks the server for a size matched to the screen and fetches the neighbouring photos in advance, so that the arrows and the slideshow no longer wait; the photo library no longer waits for its tags and people to show its albums; the backdrops of detail pages are no wider than the screen needs.
- **People on albums, photos and videos**: link people to a photo album (optionally to its whole content), to a photo or to a video: from an API source (the « cast » of an album or of a listed file), a local source (« Distribution » of an album, « Personnes » of a file) or the People screen (« Album photo », « Photo ou vidéo » by tag). Videos also get them in Jellyfin's own cast; for photos and albums, where Jellyfin keeps none, the plugin keeps the links and the web theme shows them: on the album, in the viewer, as a filter of the photo library and on the person's page. A cast list of plain names is now read too.
- **Videos in photo albums**: a video of an album gets its title, overview, date, rating, tags, genres and studio like a photo (by its file name in the album, or by its own tag). On the theme's screens photos and videos are counted apart, an album of videos gets a picture, and videos play in the viewer when the browser reads their format (else a button opens Jellyfin's player).
- **Projection theme: photo libraries**: with the theme script on, a photo library shows a wall of albums with a search and its tags and genres one click away; an album opens on its picture, whole whatever its shape, with its sheet, sub-albums and photos at their own ratios; a photo opens in a viewer with what is known of it beside it, and a slideshow.
- **Source editor: a shorter menu**: an API source now has one entry per content (« Séries et vidéos », « Albums photo »), their sections as tabs above the form.
- **Sources tab: the choices of « Actualiser » in blocks**: videos, albums and options are grouped, each choice a button that shows whether it is on.
- **Photo albums and photos**: an API or local source can now hold photo albums (« Contenu de cette source » in the editor, off by default). A folder tagged `{prefix-ID}` in a library of home videos and photos gets its title, overview, date, rating, tags, genres, studio and images, sub-albums and folders holding only sub-albums included. A photo is found by its file name in its album (no renaming) or by its own tag; what the source gives wins over the file's EXIF, the rest is kept. Local sources have an album tree with the photos of each album; the source test and the targeted refresh cover albums and photos. No cast: Jellyfin keeps none on these items.
- **Fix: Projection theme on Jellyfin 12: studios and genres were hidden** on detail pages, because the theme took the group of info rows for empty.
- **Projection theme: missing episodes are hidden on the « Index » screens**: lists and library screens now leave them out of the results and counts; a « Manquants » button in the filter bar, with their number, shows them, and the « Manquants » playback state of the filter panel still shows them alone.
- **Projection theme: the library screens take less room**: jellyfin-web's extra bar row with the view menu (Séries, Épisodes, Suggestions…) is gone and the picture moves up; the title offers the same views, and once a filter is on, a small button above it does.
- **Projection theme: the « Index » screens show their picture like a series or episode page**: the whole picture, centred, no longer cut to a low strip across the screen; its colours light both sides and tint the page, and the title sits on its lower left.
- **Fix: two pictures cut in half at the top of the « Index » screens**: with Jellyfin's « Backdrops » display setting on, its background picture showed behind the top bar, stopping where the screen's own picture began, and behind the posters too. These screens now show their own picture only.
- **Fix: clicking a genre could find nothing** once Jellyfin had removed an unused genre and it came back: its entry is created again.
- **The plugin now runs on Jellyfin 12.4, and Jellyfin 10.11 is no longer supported**: it is built for 12.4 (.NET 10) and does not load on an older server; 0.0.8 remains the last version for 10.11.6. Previews of missing episodes and episode chapters work again on Jellyfin 12. Because Jellyfin 12 refuses the old way of signing requests, the plugin's pages and the Projection theme were changed too: sending an image or a video, pictures shown in the editors, the dump export and the theme's hover preview. Also fixed: two labels were hard to read (Diagnostic page, « manquants » badge of a catalogue series).
- **Fix: the « Index » screens on Jellyfin 12**: on a Jellyfin 12 server the server search failed, so lists and libraries fell back to stock screens, and jellyfin-web 12 kept its own library toolbar in the header, showed the posters in one centred column and left an empty band under the header. The search now reads the database on 12 too, the header keeps only the library's view menu, and the grid and header fit.
- **Projection theme: library screens and fast paging**: with the theme script on, a library's Series, Episodes and Films views are now the « Index » screen too (letter column, more sort orders; the other views stay jellyfin-web's). The plugin does the filtering and counting on the server and the browser receives 60 items at a time, the next ones as you scroll: on a test library of 12 600 items a screen shows in under 2 s (reading it all took close to a minute). Each user sees only what their account allows.
- **Projection theme: genre, tag, studio and person lists, rebuilt as an « Index »**: with the theme script on, these lists now say what they show (the filter is the title, over a picture of the results; a click on a word removes it), include episodes (jellyfin-web's genre list leaves them out), sort them by type and series, and narrow down with any number of genres, tags, studios, people, years and playback states from one full-width panel with a single search field (« Tous les filtres » or `/`). Play, shuffle, the item menu, hover previews and « back » keep working. Without the script or the theme's stylesheet, the lists are jellyfin-web's own.
- **Projection theme: the person page**: the portrait, birth facts and links stay in a left column that follows the scroll, with the biography and then films, series and episodes as grids on the right; the person's backdrop is a banner behind the name. With the theme script on, the page also shows the jobs over the name, what the library holds, a count on each section title, and the backdrops take turns.
- **Episode photo gallery** (formerly the separate « Episode Gallery » plugin, now part of this one): with « Galerie photos des épisodes » on (Settings tab, off by default), an episode page gets a « Photos » button opening a wall of its photos at their own ratios, then a full-size viewer lit by the photo’s colours (keyboard, swipe). Photos come from the new « Photo » type of a local source’s episode gallery (also shown on missing episodes) and from the sub-folder named after the episode number next to the video (`03`, `03-04`); they are offered in « Modifier les images » → Rechercher too. Photo folders no longer turn into empty seasons. Disable or uninstall the old plugin.
- **Projection theme: a redesigned preview viewer**: the « Aperçu » of an episode opens in a dark room lit by the video's own colours, with the theme's controls (play, position, sound, full screen) in place of the browser's, the series, season and episode under it, and a new « Lire l'épisode » button (« Reprendre l'épisode » when it was started) that plays the episode itself. The bar is cut into the 8 moments of a clip built by the plugin (the arrows jump from one to the next) and runs in one piece for a source's video; when that video cannot be played, the plugin's clip takes over.
- **Fix: an episode's page shows its own picture again**: since the backdrops came first, an episode's header showed its series' backdrop, the same on every episode. The episode's still now comes first again when it is sharp enough; otherwise the series' backdrop fills the header and the still stands in the poster's place under it (on phones, above the facts), so the episode is recognised. Hovering that still plays the episode's preview in it, like an episode card, and a click opens the preview large when the source or the plugin gives one.
- **Fix: smaller landscape backdrops show in the Projection header**: a backdrop under 1200 px wide (a 1080x720 one, for example) was turned down as not sharp enough and the header fell back to another picture or the poster. A backdrop is now kept when it is sharp enough for the size it is actually shown at on that screen (up to a third of upscaling, never under 640 px).
- **Fix: the Projection theme's picture header stays when you go back to a page**: returning to an episode, season, series or movie page already visited (back button, or a link to it again) showed it without its header until a reload.
- **Projection theme: an ambient light beside a header picture narrower than the screen**: the margins beside the backdrop no longer copy its edge colours; they glow with soft clouds of the picture's own colours (its sides, top, middle and bottom), like YouTube's ambient mode and the portrait hover previews, and the light drifts to the next picture's colours when the backdrops take turns.
- **Fix: the hover preview of an episode you have uses the video of your local source**: a preview typed or uploaded on a local episode (over an API source's episode, or in a local source of its own) was ignored when the season's list came from elsewhere, so the plugin's generated clip played instead. It now wins, and a change applies without waiting.
- **Projection theme: hover previews play the episode**: hovering an episode plays a muted video, its preview trailer for a missing episode, else a few seconds taken at five points of its own file; trickplay thumbnails are only the fallback when the browser cannot play the file, and then use the sharpest set for the frame and show their progress as steps. A preview keeps its own shape (a portrait video or thumbnail is shown whole, no longer stretched) and drops the dimmed look of missing and watched episodes while it plays.
- **Fix: uploaded images rotated by their EXIF tag no longer turn back once selected**: a picture rotated in a viewer often only gets an EXIF "Orientation" tag; the browser honours it (the magnifier of « Modifier les images » showed it landscape) but Jellyfin's resizer ignores it, so the thumbnails, the selected image and the Projection header showed it unrotated (portrait). The plugin now rotates such images for real before handing them to Jellyfin. Images already applied are fixed with « Appliquer les images ».
- **Fix: the « Appliquer les images » button shows again** in the Images tab of a local source element: its block showed only its text, the button being hidden by a style rule of the editor.
- **Images removed from a source leave Jellyfin**: « Actualiser les éléments » with « Remplacer les images » (and « Appliquer les images ») now removes from Jellyfin the images the plugin applied earlier and the source no longer gives, for example every backdrop removed from a local source. Only images the plugin applied itself are removed: one added by hand or by another provider stays. Images applied before this version are not known and have to be removed once by hand.
- **Fix: the progress bar of a portrait preview stays visible**: it went behind the video, so only its ends showed on the sides.
- **Projection theme: backdrops first, as a slideshow, without a hard edge**: the poster header now takes the item's backdrops (else its parent's) before an episode's still or a thumbnail, and when there are several it cross-fades between them every 9 s, the page's colours following; on wide screens the picture's sides melt into the margins, which also darken towards the bottom like the picture, so its frame no longer shows as a distinct rectangle.
- **« Aperçu » button on episodes that have a preview**: an episode with a file whose source gives a preview video (or with a clip built by the plugin) gets a « film » button next to « Lire » on its page and on its row of the season page; it opens the preview large, with sound and controls (Escape, the close button or a click outside closes it). Where a preview comes from is always shown: in the viewer and the button's tooltip (« Aperçu de la source « Nom » » or « Extraits créés par le plugin à partir du fichier »), and as a corner chip on hover previews (« Aperçu de la source », « Extraits (plugin) », « Fichier de l'épisode », « Vignettes Jellyfin »). Missing episodes keep playing their preview through their play button.
- **Fix: a source's preview video is no longer skipped on hover**: the preview gave up after 4 s without playing and fell back to the episode's file, while a preview host or the first look-up of a season's episode list can take longer; it now waits for the video (a missing video or an unsupported codec still falls back at once). The look-up also finds episodes whose season folder carries no tag (the season item's identifier) and files joining several episodes (each of their tags).
- **Preview clips built by the plugin, and an ambient light around portrait previews**: a new scheduled task « Générer les aperçus des épisodes » (every night, only with the theme script on) uses ffmpeg to build, for each episode whose source has no preview video, a small silent clip of 8 moments of 1.5 s spread over the episode (360 px, about 0.5 MB, rebuilt when the file changes, removed when it leaves the library); the hover preview plays it after the source's preview and before the file itself, and its progress bar runs straight through the episode. A portrait preview now has an ambient light like YouTube's: soft semi-transparent clouds of the video's own colours that drift slowly from one colour to the next, instead of side bands.
- **Hover preview uses the source's preview video for episodes that have their file too**: the plugin looks the episode's `preview.url` up in its season's episode list (kept 30 minutes) and streams it through the server (`CustomMetadata/Preview/{id}`: the source's headers stay on the server, seeking works); the episode's own file is only used when its source has no preview and the file is light (12 Mb/s at most), then trickplay.
- **Projection theme: movies on the « Affiche » too, finished**: a movie's poster header shows the critics' score next to the rating, drops the « (2021) » year from the title (it is in the facts), lines up the poster-only layout's actions and texts with the title column, and the director and writers become chips like the genres (tappable on phones).
- **Fix: after a plugin update the theme's CSS and script stay in step**: the served theme files are revalidated on every load instead of being cached for 5 minutes; a browser kept the old CSS with the new script, and the new header showed unstyled (icons out of place, text off centre).
- **Projection theme: seasons and episodes shown as a film poster too**: the same « Affiche » header in its three cases; the series' logo (or name) links back to the series, then « S1 · É3 » and the title in the serif; the poster is the season's own, or the series' on an episode; the billing gives the number of episodes and what is left to watch for a season, the air date, runtime and « Réalisé par / Écrit par / Avec » for an episode.
- **Projection theme: series and movies shown as a film poster (« Affiche »)** (with the theme script): with a landscape backdrop, the picture whole and centred, the logo (or the title) on its lower third, the series' poster standing on its lower edge and a poster's billing block under it (tagline, « … présentent » credits from studios, cast and creators, status, years, seasons, rating); with only a poster, the poster large on a band lit by its own colours, the billing beside it; with no picture at all, a typographic poster (the title set large, an amber rule, the billing).
- **Projection theme: a header built around the item's picture** (with the theme script): the backdrop is shown whole at its own ratio, as large as the screen allows, and the page takes its colours: the margin beside it continues its left edge band by band, the ground below is a deep shade of its bottom edge, the text sits on a scrim of its own dark colour (no blur, no black veil). Portrait backdrops are skipped (next landscape backdrop, then the thumbnail, else the compact header); the item's logo replaces its title, the series' logo its name above seasons and episodes, and a series shows its status (« En cours », « Terminée »).
- **Projection theme script** (optional): the new setting « Améliorations du thème Projection » makes the plugin add the theme's script to jellyfin-web's pages (inserted as the page is served, nothing written to Jellyfin's files). It brings a hover preview of episodes (trickplay thumbnails scrolled, or the preview video of a missing episode), « S1 · É3 » and previous / next links on episode pages, the episode's own picture as the backdrop when it is large enough, the current episode marked in « Plus de Saison N », a series' progress (« 625 / 1 076 vus · 17 manquants »), « Reprendre · S27 É2 » on its play button, a bar on each season started, and episode numbers on season pages.
- **Projection theme: the backdrop is shown whole, at its own shape**: jellyfin-web stretched it over the whole screen and the header only showed its top half; on detail pages it now sits on the right of the header at its own ratio (3:2, 16:9...: measured by the theme script, never cropped or stretched without it) and melts into the ink on its left and bottom edges, the title and synopsis on its left.
- **Projection theme: episode page**: the veil under the title and synopsis now scrolls with them (it stayed on the screen, and the scrolled synopsis went grey on the bright sky of the backdrop); an episode's picture is larger and aligned with the title block (on phones it replaces the band, full width); the file's tracks (« Vidéo 240p H264 ») move under the tags, smaller; in « Plus de Saison N » the watched episodes recede. The theme audit now measures contrast on the screenshot on every screen, which catches light text over a bright picture.
- **The plugin serves the Projection theme**: with the plugin installed, the Custom CSS line `@import url("../CustomMetadata/Theme/projection.css");` loads the copy of the theme embedded in the plugin, so the theme changes with the plugin version instead of waiting for jsDelivr's cache or for the next release on `main`; the jsDelivr import still works without the plugin.
- **Projection theme: detail pages as a compact sheet, seasons as a poster wall**: the header adapts to the item: with a backdrop, a band of about half a screen shows the picture clear, veiled only under the title and synopsis; without one, a compact header (no empty band) where poster, title, play button, synopsis and tags sit together; a series' seasons become large posters (7-8 a row at 1920, 3 on phones) with their state as a chip and the season to resume ringed in amber, and watched seasons shrink to small posters in place, so on a series with dozens of seasons (One Piece's arcs) the one to resume comes up the page; a season without an image keeps its name inside the poster.
- **Projection theme: second pass**: the experimental layout's header turns solid once the page scrolls (scrolled titles no longer show through it); a series or season without a backdrop gets a short band instead of half a screen of empty ink; missing episodes carry « Manquant » on their picture and no longer show an "ends at" time; on phones the episode play button sits in the corner of the picture again instead of covering its middle, the A-Z index of a library fits under the header, and card play buttons use the theme's chip; a desktop browser in a narrow window no longer squeezes the detail page into a column a few words wide; the synopsis is larger and closer to the actions; « Supprimer » stands out in red in the item menu. The end of a detail page's text (last lines of the synopsis, tags, genres) is no longer darkened by the fade into the next section, which made it barely visible on a backdrop; tags, genres and studios are shown as chips (tappable on phones). Also: « Voir plus » follows the synopsis instead of floating at the far right; a page no longer scrolls sideways when a row of cards is wider than the screen (home at 1024, search: a jellyfin-web bug); empty Favourites explain how to add one instead of a blank page; season and episode cards no longer stretch to ~740px when alone on their row; on phones a long synopsis stops at four lines. `tools/ui-test/theme-seed.mjs` gives the test server varied data and `theme-audit.mjs` checks every screen (text darkened by a layer, contrast, overlaps, sideways scroll, cut text, tap size, focus) in six layouts and widths. `tools/ui-test/theme-shots.mjs` takes screenshots of the theme in the test container.
- **Fix: a series is identified again from its folder name**: the series provider now reads the `{prefix-id}` tag of the series' own folder first, like the season provider. Before, it only used the stored `custom` id, then the item's name, which becomes the API title once the series is enriched: a series whose `custom` id had been cleared (metadata editor, Identify…) could never be enriched again although its folder still carried the tag.
- **Fix: a missing episode keeps its own number even when a real file has it**: the number typed in the list (local source or API) is now always the episode number in Jellyfin. Before, when a real episode of the series already had that number, the missing episode was moved to another one (44, 45, 46… behind the episodes of other seasons without a number, because Jellyfin groups those seasons together), and the screen asked to rename the season folder. The missing episode now goes to a group of its own inside its season, so Jellyfin keeps it, in its place; nothing to rename. The card warning only remains for a number the list gives to two episodes.
- **Fix: missing episodes no longer keep numbers in the millions**: when a real episode held their number (Jellyfin reads numbers from file names, tags included: `Bonus {ab-1-6149542}.mkv` is episode 6149542), the missing ones got 6149543, 6149544… and the next analyses reused these numbers from the cache, so the earlier fixes did not change them. Such numbers are no longer reused, an episode with its own number takes the first free one after it, and the « Épisodes manquants » screen now says on the card which file holds the number and which number Jellyfin shows instead.
- **Fix: a missing episode always keeps the number chosen for it**: the number is now written back after the episode's refresh (read from the library, whatever another provider wrote in the meantime) and the metadata provider gives it back when Jellyfin refreshes a missing episode. An episode left with a wrong number is set right at the next analysis, and the log says so (`🔎 [MISSING] ⚠️`).
- **Fix: a missing episode no longer jumps to a huge number**: when the number of a missing episode is already held by a real episode (Jellyfin reads numbers from file names: `Clip 09.mkv` is episode 9, `Video 6149542.mkv` is 6149), the virtual episode took the highest real number + 1, which could be in the millions. It now takes the next free number after its own, real numbers above 10000 are ignored for the fallback, and the log says which file holds the number (`🔎 [MISSING] ⚠️`).
- **A people source can read the networks from a list of objects**: when the API gives them as `"profileSocials": [{ "providerId": "1", "handle": "name1" }, …]`, the « Personne » pane of the source now takes the path of the list, the path of the network identifier and of the handle inside each item, and a table linking each identifier value (1, 4, …) to a network of the Personnes screen. Identifiers with no link are listed by the source's test and when you press « Compléter », so you know which ones are left to link.
- **Fix: the buttons of a person's entry work again**: since the networks tab, « Compléter », « Écraser avec l'API », « Retirer » and « Ajouter un identifiant », and the photo buttons (« Envoyer un fichier… », « Ajuster », « Retirer ») did nothing in the Personnes screen.
- **Fix: the links of a person's page follow the order of the networks list**: Jellyfin sorts its link providers by name once at startup, so the links appeared in the alphabetical order of the names (emoji included). The plugin's link slots now answer a fixed sort key until the server has started, then their real name: the order set with ▲ ▼ in the « Réseaux » tab is the order shown on the person's page.
- **A network can have a real image icon (SVG, PNG, ICO, favicon)**: in the « Réseaux » tab, each network can take an uploaded image (256 KB max, SVG with scripts refused) or fetch the favicon of its site. The plugin serves a style sheet (`/CustomMetadata/People/Networks/Icons.css`, anonymous) to import once in Jellyfin's custom CSS: it puts the image in front of the network's links on the person page of the web client. A network with an image no longer has its emoji in the link name; the others keep it. A network whose address template starts with `{value}` (no fixed host) cannot get an image.
- **People: networks have their own tab, and a people source can fill backdrops and links**: the list of networks is now edited in a « Réseaux » tab of the Personnes screen (icon, displayed name, address template with a live preview, order, number of people using it, delete, reset to the defaults). A people source has two new optional mappings: « Images de fond » (a path to a list of images or a single one, completed like the photo) and « Réseaux et liens » (one path per network of the list). « Compléter » and « Écraser avec l'API » bring them into the person's entry, and the source's test shows them.
- **People: photos are copied into the plugin, plus background images and several links**: a person's photo (or background image) given as a web address is now downloaded and stored by the plugin when you save the directory, so an address that expires (signed link) no longer breaks it; an image that cannot be fetched keeps its address. A person can have **background images** (Backdrop) that are written on their Jellyfin page with « Appliquer aux épisodes », and **several links on different networks** (Instagram, IMDb, …): a global, editable list of networks (name, emoji icon, address template with `{value}`; up to 24, edited from « Gérer les réseaux… ») feeds the external links of the person page in Jellyfin, the icon preceding the link name. Stored in `people.json` and `person-networks.json`.
- **A dump season gets virtual episodes like any other source**: a season folder tagged `{prefix-seriesId}` of a dump source (the series id of « Série : identifiant ») is now analysed like an API season: the dump entries of that series that you do not own are listed in the « Séries » view of Épisodes manquants and get their virtual episode, thumbnail, « Ignorer » and tab counter, next to the other sources' missing episodes. Nothing to configure beyond the series id path; a season tagged with an id the dump does not know is left alone. A series folder alone (no season) is only linked on the « Catalogues » screen.
- **The dump's « Catalogues » screen no longer recomputes everything at each click**: what it works out (the entries linked to your library, the totals, groups, tags, years and series groups) is kept and reused until the downloaded dump is replaced (reload or `CacheMinutes`), the source's settings change, or the library changes (a file or a series/season added, removed or updated; a 10-minute expiry covers a missed event). Preview pictures are looked up in an index instead of rebuilding every entry for each picture. The first display after one of those events computes again, as before.
- **A dump series is linked to its season or series in the library**: when the series id of a dump series is the `{prefix-id}` tag of a season (or series) folder of the library, the series is shown as « ✓ Dans votre médiathèque » even before any of its entries is owned, and its page offers « Ouvrir la saison (la série) dans Jellyfin ↗ ». A season wins over a series with the same tag.
- **Series page of a dump has a recap with copy buttons**: opening a series on the « Catalogues » screen now shows a recap like a season's page (picture, name, library link, missing badge, progress, entries owned), a « Copier les N noms » button (the entries shown, one name per line, filters and page applied) and, when the dump gives a series id, a « Tag de la série » line with a « Copier le tag » button (`{prefix-seriesId}`).
- **Copy the tag of a series or a season (Épisodes manquants)**: when the series list of a source is opened, its `{prefix-id}` tag is shown with a « Copier le tag » button, and the season / source page shows both the season's and the series' tags with the same button, the way file names are already copied. The series tag is read from its folder name (or its stored id) at the next analysis; nothing is shown when a series has none.
- **Dump catalogue reads tags like the mapping does**: the tag list and tag filter of the « Catalogues » screen ignored a tags path written `tags[].label` and the « Champ de chaque tag » setting, so tags that are a list of objects never showed. They are now read exactly as the video mapping reads them (`tags`, `tags[].label`, `a || b`, comma-separated text, item field).
- **Click-to-fill in a dump's Catalogue section**: after « Tester » on an entry, clicking a value in the result now writes its path into the Catalogue field you are in (id, name, slug, other titles, group, series, likes…), as it already did in the mapping sections. The list-of-entries path is left out: it is read on the whole response, which the entry result does not show.
- **Series id for a dump**: two new optional paths in the Catalogue section, « Série : identifiant » and « Série : nom ». Entries that share the series id form one series on the « Catalogues » screen, instead of being guessed from titles that only differ by a trailing number; the name comes from the name path, or else from the titles they share. An entry without a series id keeps the grouping by title.
- **The test panel scrolls on its own**: in a source's editor, the « Tester la source » panel stays in view while the form scrolls and now has a maximum height with its own scrollbar, so a long result (a raw response, a list of candidates) can be read to the end without losing the form's position.
- **Readable raw response, also in the mapping test**: the raw response shown when a dump cannot be read is now indented JSON (long lists cut to their first two elements, so the structure is visible), and « Lire une entrée avec les mappings » (with or without an id) shows the same explanation and raw response instead of a bare error.
- **Dump test shows what the API answered**: when the list of entries is not found, the message now says where the lists of the response are (« Listes trouvées : « results » (100 éléments) », or that the response is itself a list), and a « Réponse brute de l'API (début) » block shows the start of the response (or the HTTP error and its body). A failed reload that fell back to an older copy is now reported as a failure in the test instead of looking successful.
- **Dump test without an id**: the « Tester » button of a dump source's editor now works with an empty identifier and reads the first entry of the dump through the mappings, so the mappings can be checked without knowing an id.

## Version 0.0.5 alpha (February 2026)

### 🐛 CRITICAL BUG FIXED - ID extraction from the full path

**The problem:** every provider looked for `{PREFIX-ID}` in the **FULL path** instead of the file/folder name only, so they always found the **FIRST ID** in the path.

**Example:**
```
Path: /var/media/Series {mv-S123}/Season {mv-SOURCE}/Episode {mv-EP001}.mkv

BEFORE:
- Series provider  → searches the whole path → finds "S123" ✅
- Season provider  → searches the whole path → finds "S123" ❌ (should be "SOURCE")
- Episode provider → searches the whole path → finds "S123" ❌ (should be "EP001")
```

**The fix:** `Path.GetFileName()` extracts **only the last segment** of the path; each provider now searches its own file or folder name only.

```
- Series provider  → searches "Series {mv-S123}"          → finds "S123" ✅
- Season provider  → searches "Season {mv-SOURCE}"        → finds "SOURCE" ✅
- Episode provider → searches "Episode {mv-EP001}.mkv"    → finds "EP001" ✅
```

### ✨ Logging improvements

**One emoji per provider:**
- 📺 `[SERIES PROVIDER]` - series logs
- 📁 `[SEASON PROVIDER]` - season logs
- 🎬 `[EPISODE PROVIDER]` - episode logs

**Detailed extraction logs:**
```
📺 [SERIES PROVIDER] Extracted folder name: My Series {mv-S123} from path: /var/media/...
📺 [SERIES PROVIDER] ✅ Extracted series ID 'S123' from folder name
```

### 🔧 Other fixes

- **Stricter regex**: captures only valid IDs (letters, digits, hyphens, underscores)
  - BEFORE: `\{mv-([^}]+)\}` captured EVERYTHING between the braces (`{mv-123 with text}` → `"123 with text"` ❌)
  - NOW: `\{mv-([\w\-]+)\}` captures valid characters only (`{mv-123}` → `"123"` ✅, `{mv-123 text}` → no match)
- Added manual ID extraction for series (fallback when `CustomExternalId` does not work)
- Better logs to trace exactly what is extracted and from where
- Logs showing the regex pattern used and the exact match

```
Tags: tagList
Tag field: label
```

**Both behave the same**; the `[]` notation is more intuitive.

**Supported formats:**
- ✅ Array of strings: `["fantasy", "sci-fi"]`
- ✅ Array of objects: `[{"label": "fantasy"}, {"id": 123, "label": "sci-fi"}]`
- ✅ Comma-separated string: `"fantasy, sci-fi"`
- ✅ JSONPath-like notation: `tagList[].label`, `metadata.tags[].name`

**Supported JSON example:**
```json
{
  "tagList": [
    {"id": 4420, "count": 1, "label": "fantasy"},
    {"id": 4421, "count": 5, "label": "sci-fi"}
  ]
}
```
